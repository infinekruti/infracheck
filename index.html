Methodology

Specialized NDT Methods We Perform

Standardized execution adhering strictly to ASME Section V, ASTM, and ISO parameters.

Ultrasonic Testing UT

High-frequency sound waves evaluate internal discontinuities, weld defects, laminations, and precision wall thickness gauging.

Radiographic Testing RT

X-Ray and Gamma-Ray radiography revealing volumetric flaws like porosity, slag inclusion, and lack of penetration in welds.

Magnetic Particle MPT / MPI

Detects surface and near-surface cracks in ferromagnetic materials including welds, forged shafts, and structural beams.

Liquid Penetrant DPT / PT

Identifies surface-breaking flaws, leaks, and pinholes across non-porous metals, stainless steels, and exotic alloys.

Visual Inspection VT

Direct visual and optical borescope checks verifying weld profiles, alignment, root pass fusion, and surface corrosion.

Positive Material ID PMI

Fast on-site XRF elemental analysis ensuring precise alloy grade verification and preventing material mix-ups.
